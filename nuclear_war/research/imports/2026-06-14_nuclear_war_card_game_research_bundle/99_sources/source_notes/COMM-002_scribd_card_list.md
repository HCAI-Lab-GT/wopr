# COMM-002 - Scribd Nuclear War Game Card List

URL: https://www.scribd.com/document/401221633/Nuclear-War-Card-List-pdf

Classification: third-party hosted card list. It appears to preserve older Flying Buffalo-era card-list content, but the page is hosted on Scribd and marks rights as reserved.

Key facts captured:

- Lists Nuclear War Basics, Nuclear Escalation, Nuclear Proliferation, WMD Cardlist, and Population Decks.
- Gives common warhead/propaganda counts.
- Explains edition/card-color identification changes through December 2001.
- Gives card names/counts for multiple sets.
- Gives population deck distribution:
  - ten 1M cards;
  - ten 2M cards;
  - ten 5M cards;
  - six 10M cards;
  - four 25M cards.

Use as a card-name and count source. Do not redistribute exact card text from Scribd; capture only structured inventory unless you have rights or a physical-copy transcription.
