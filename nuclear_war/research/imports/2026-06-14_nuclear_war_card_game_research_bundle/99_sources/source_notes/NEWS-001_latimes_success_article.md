# NEWS-001 - Los Angeles Times, "Nuclear War Game Bombs Its Way to Success"

URL: https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1986-04-18-vw-442-story.html

Classification: historical news article.

Key facts captured:

- Article reported Rick Loomis bought exclusive licensing rights from Malewicki in 1972.
- Article reported approximately 60,000 copies sold by 1986 at a $14.95 price point.
- Article described older copies as coming with two decks of cards and a spinner.
- It listed spinner categories such as dud warhead, bomb shelters saving 2 million, and radioactive fallout killing another 2 million.
- It described convention/tournament culture and mentions a group of officers playing the game at the Pentagon during lunch breaks.
- It records controversy and the black-humor/satire framing.

Use for cultural/history context and old spinner confirmation, not for rules implementation except as corroboration.
