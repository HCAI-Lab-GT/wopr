# COMM-003 - Scribd Nuclear War FAQ

URL: https://www.scribd.com/document/401221755/Nuclear-War-FAQs

Classification: third-party hosted FAQ/rules clarification page. It appears to preserve an older Flying Buffalo-era FAQ, but exact hosting/provenance should be verified if used as authority.

Key facts captured:

- Page says it was last updated September 2, 2008.
- FAQ says the default official answer to unresolved disputes is that the owner of the game decides.
- Gives deck composition guidance:
  - Original game: about 34% megatons, 27% weapons, 4% antimissiles, 20% propaganda, 15% secrets.
  - Suggested custom decks: roughly 30% warheads, 20% weapons/carriers, 20% propaganda, 4% ABMs, and about 26% secrets/specials.
- Discusses new cards including Advanced Recycling, Fold in Space, and Mad Scientist.
- Includes many edge-case clarifications for final retaliation, anti-missiles, submarines, Black Box ECM, MX missile handling, Civil Defense, bomb shelters, and booster interactions.
- Confirms a 40-card population deck distribution in the Scribd search result excerpt.

Use for rules-engine edge cases, not as final exact card text.
