# UNOFF-001 - Variable Pig Nuclear War postal rules

URL: https://variablepig.org/rules/nuclearwar.html

Classification: unofficial / postal-by-mail / press variant.

Key facts captured:

- Richard Smith's 1998 postal rules are based on Keith Dowsett's rules and earlier *Shadows of Amber* postal rules.
- Supports 2 to 8 players.
- Can use *Nuclear War*, *Nuclear Escalation*, and *Nuclear Proliferation* cards.
- Recommends no trading, no special powers, and removal of secrets that make players miss turns.
- Because play is simultaneous/postal, secrets and final retaliation are delayed or handled differently from tabletop play.
- Press is explicitly encouraged and includes public statements, treaties, rumors, deterrent announcements, and libelous/funny public diplomacy.
- This is the best source found for a press/no-press simulation variant.

Modeling implication:

Treat press as a communication layer. It changes diplomacy, commitment signaling, and deception but does not need to change core blast-resolution mechanics unless the GM/order-resolution rules explicitly do so.
