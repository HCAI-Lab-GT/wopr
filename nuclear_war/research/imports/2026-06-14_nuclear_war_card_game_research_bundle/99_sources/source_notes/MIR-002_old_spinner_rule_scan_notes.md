# MIR-002 - Older spinner-era Nuclear War rule-sheet scan

URL: https://magisterrex.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/nuclear-war-rules.pdf
Captured file: `nuclear-war-rules_magisterrex_mirror.pdf` (not redistributed)

This is a two-page scan. Text extraction did not work, so the notes below were made by visual inspection of the rendered pages.

Important old-edition details visible in the scan:

- Header: *Nuclear War*, for 2 to 6 players; copyright 1965 by Douglas Malewicki.
- Preparation uses population-card counts by player count:
  - 2 players: 15 population cards each.
  - 3 players: 10 cards each.
  - 4 players: 8 cards each.
  - 5 players: 7 cards each.
  - 6 players: 6 cards each.
- The owner of the game handles the initial Secret/Top Secret resolution and then begins play.
- Each player places two face-down strategy cards before normal play begins.
- War is declared when a player reveals a warhead card preceded by an appropriate missile or bomber card and announces a target.
- Attack resolution uses a physical Radioactive Fallout dial/spinner.
- Anti-missile defense occurs after the attack is announced but before the spinner is used.
- Propaganda is ineffective during war and discarded without effect; peace returns after a player is removed from the game.
- Final retaliation is available when eliminated by secret/top-secret effects or nuclear weapons, but not after peaceful propaganda defeat.
- Components list visible in the scan:
  - 1 rules sheet.
  - 1 spinner.
  - 1 deck of population cards, 40 cards total.
  - 2 decks of missile and warhead cards, 100 cards total.
  - 2 strategy mats.
- Replacement-parts list visible in the scan includes rules sheet, spinner, population deck, complete set of 100 missile/warhead cards, supergerm T-shirt, bumper sticker, and population bumper sticker.

Use this source to reconcile spinner-era components and layout. For exact text, inspect the scan or transcribe from the physical copy.
