# Official Nuclear Destruction summary

Source: official Mr. B Games PDFs (OFF-002 through OFF-004 in `source_index.csv`).

*Nuclear Destruction* is the currently hosted official Nuclear War-family implementation. It reimplements *Weapons of Mass Destruction* and provides a current ruleset with six-player standalone support.

Key mechanics retained/modernized:

- population as win/loss condition;
- propaganda as peaceful population-stealing mechanism;
- warheads + delivery systems;
- anti-missile/countermeasure defense;
- launch-track commitment;
- Secrets and Top Secrets;
- deterrent force display;
- spinner-based strike modification;
- optional Nuclear Escalation die;
- final strike and chain-elimination risk;
- everyone-can-lose ending.

Important official optional rules:

- **More people**: give each player a 25M population card before dealing and deal one fewer population card afterward.
- **Not so secretive**: if a player is initially dealt more than two secrets, keep two random secrets, bottom-deck the rest, and draw replacements.
- **Bombers aren't quite as fast as Missiles**: bomber targeting is initially restricted to adjacent players, then may move onward if not intercepted.

Combination guidance from the official rules recommends, for a double-sized deck suitable up to eight players:

- 60 delivery systems;
- 40 propaganda cards;
- 70 warheads;
- 40 secrets/specials, no more than 10 top secrets;
- 6 anti-missiles;
- 4 other cards such as hybrids or extra specials/secrets.
