# Exact text gap list

The following need physical-copy or authorized-source verification:

1. Exact wording of every base *Nuclear War* card.
2. Exact wording of every Secret and Top Secret card.
3. Exact wording and capacity values of delivery systems across editions.
4. Exact anti-missile card text in the base game and any edition-specific target lists.
5. Exact spinner layout/probabilities for the user's edition.
6. Whether the user's copy uses 20 or 40 population cards, and exact population distribution.
7. Whether the user's copy uses the physical spinner, the two-d10 chart, or both.
8. Whether the user's deck includes Advanced Recycling, Mars Probe, Fold in Space, Mad Scientist, booster cards, or 50th Anniversary cards.
9. Exact B-70 bomber payload handling as printed in the user's rules/cards.
10. Any edition-specific rules on hand size, initial draw, and how cards on mats count toward hand limit.

## Recommended physical-copy capture protocol

- Photograph each unique card front and back.
- Sort by deck/back/color identifier.
- Count duplicates by card name.
- Photograph rules sheet, spinner, mats, and population cards.
- Photograph the spinner straight-on at high resolution.
- Create a CSV with one row per unique card and one `count` field.
- Have a second pass verify counts and ambiguous words.
