# Variant Acceptance Criteria

This page records what I need before treating an edition or variant as active runtime behavior.
It defines acceptance criteria only. It does not verify source material or enable deferred
variants.

Deferred variants remain rejected until their blockers are resolved and these criteria are
covered by tests, replay validation, and source evidence.

## base_later_two_d10

Status: active.

- 10-card hand draw target verified.
- 40-card population deck verified.
- Two-d10 fallout chart verified.
- Initial face-down setup verified.
- Press and expansion behavior remain disabled.

## classic_spinner_scan

Status: deferred.

- Spinner probabilities transcribed from a physical copy or authorized source.
- 9-card hand model verified.
- Classic deck boundary verified.

## nuclear_destruction_modern

- Nuclear Escalation die rules verified.
- Six-player mat rules verified.

## postal_press

## no_press_house
